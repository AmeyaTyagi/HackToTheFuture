Vision test

About a minute. Photon uses your answers to set itself up for your eyes.

1Each plate is a circle of dots that may hide a single number.
2Press the number you see, or say that you can't see one.
3Don't study a plate for long. Your first impression is the right answer.
4Turn off Night Light, True Tone or any blue-light filter first, and set the screen to at least half brightness.
5The plates get harder as you get them right, so expect a few you can't read.

Color correction is paused while this window is open.

Which number do you see?

This is a screening aid, not a medical diagnosis. Screen brightness, room lighting and display quality all affect it. An optometrist can test your color vision properly.
